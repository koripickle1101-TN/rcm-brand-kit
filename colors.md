# Color System

## Primary Palette

White FFFFFF

Use for page backgrounds, section backgrounds, high whitespace portfolio pages, and premium editorial layouts.

Black 111111

Use for primary text, main headings, strong statements, and footer text.

Charcoal 333333

Use for secondary text, body copy, supporting labels, and explanatory sections.

Tennessee Orange FF8200

Use as the main accent color. This should appear in thin dividers, section numbers, small icons, callout lines, buttons, and strategic emphasis.

Warm Gray F6F1EB

Use for soft section blocks, background structure, card fills, and subtle separation.

Champagne F6EDE7

Use sparingly for feminine executive warmth, soft highlight cards, or premium editorial accents.

## Legacy Support Colors

Smokey Gray 4B4B4B

Use for older Vol Summit assets, muted text, and secondary structure.

Deep Black 0A0A0A

Use for dark background assets, LinkedIn technical visuals, or high contrast hero sections.

## Color Rules

Use white as the default background.

Use Tennessee Orange as an accent, not as a full page color.

Use black for authority.

Use charcoal for readability.

Use warm gray for structure.

Use champagne only when the project needs a softer executive look.

Do not use random blues, purples, reds, or greens unless the project has a specific reason.

Do not overload the page with orange.

Do not use heavy gradients.

Do not use neon colors in this brand kit unless creating a separate AI strategy asset.

## Recommended Ratio

White 70 percent

Black and charcoal 20 percent

Warm gray and champagne 7 percent

Tennessee Orange 3 percent
